Questions

Does Purr need an account?

No account, no email, no phone number. It works offline.

Can I export my codes?

Yes. Plaintext otpauth:// or encrypted, any time.

What if I lose my phone?

Twelve printed words restore every code on a new one.

Can you see my codes?

No. Your secrets are encrypted on your device. Our relay only carries ciphertext.

Switching from Authy?

Authy has no export, so those entries get re-added from each service. Google Authenticator, Aegis, 2FAS and Ente import directly.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Open source, no ads, no trackers.
