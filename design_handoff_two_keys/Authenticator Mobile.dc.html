5Camera and notification permissions

Both permissions follow the same three-beat rule: never ask cold, ask at the moment the person wants the thing, and always leave a way to finish the job if they say no. 5a–5c camera, asked when they tap Scan, never at launch. 5d–5g notifications, deferred until the first account exists and the extension is paired — the point at which a push actually has something to say. Each pair shows our priming screen, the system sheet it triggers, and the denied fallback. Build note: 5b, 5e and 5g are the OS’s own alerts, drawn here only for context — build them by calling the platform permission API, never as a look-alike we render ourselves. Only the title/body wording of 5b is ours (the Info.plist purpose string); everything else in those three frames is Apple’s and Google’s to draw.
